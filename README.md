# Teranga video assets

Short synthetic Wolof audio lines used to lip-sync AI avatars in a demo video of Teranga, a voice assistant that speaks Wolof.

- All voices are **synthetic**, generated by our Teranga TTS. No real person recorded these lines.
- Voice prompts: [WaxalNLP](https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/WaxalNLP) (CC BY-SA 4.0) and
  [FLEURS](https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/fleurs) wo_sn (CC BY 4.0).
- The faces in the video are AI-generated and do not depict real people.

Audio in this repository: CC BY-SA 4.0.
